Renters Insurance

For the price of a couple of coffees a month, renters insurance protects everything you own and shields you from liability claims. Most Ontario landlords now require it, and smart tenants want it anyway.

Coverage breakdown

What's Covered

Contents

Clothes, furniture, electronics, bikes, everything you own, wherever it is.

Tenant Liability

If you accidentally cause damage to the building or a neighbour's unit, like a kitchen fire or overflow.

Additional Living Expenses

Hotel costs if a fire or flood forces you out of your rental.

Low Cost

Most tenants pay well under $30/month, and bundling with auto drops it further.

Landlord Requirements

Proof-of-insurance documents prepared for your landlord or property manager.

Scheduled Valuables

Scheduled coverage for jewelry, cameras, instruments and high-value items.

Ontario specifics

What Ontario Drivers & Owners Should Know

Ontario leases increasingly require tenants to carry liability insurance, your landlord's policy never covers your belongings. Claims like apartment fires and water leaks from neighbouring units are common in GTA rentals, and without your own policy you have zero protection for your stuff or temporary housing.

Who this is for

  • Apartment and basement tenants across Ontario
  • Students renting near GTA campuses
  • Anyone whose lease requires proof of insurance
  • Roommates wanting individual contents protection
