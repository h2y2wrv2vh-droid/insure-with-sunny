Condo Insurance

Your condo corporation's master policy doesn't cover your unit the way you think. Condo owner insurance fills the gaps, your improvements, your belongings, and surprise special assessments.

Coverage breakdown

What's Covered

Unit Improvements

Flooring, kitchens, bathrooms and upgrades, covered beyond the corporation's standard unit definition.

Contents

Everything you own inside the unit, from furniture to electronics.

Loss Assessment

Protection if the condo corp levies a special assessment after a major claim.

Personal Liability

Coverage if you cause damage to neighbouring units, like a leak into the unit below.

Additional Living Expenses

Temporary housing if your unit is uninhabitable after an insured loss.

Water Damage

Unit-owner water endorsements for the leaks and overflows condos are known for.

Ontario specifics

What Ontario Drivers & Owners Should Know

Ontario's Condo Act leaves a grey zone between the corporation's policy and yours, the 'standard unit' definition in your condo's by-laws decides where their coverage ends. Special assessments after big building claims can run into the thousands per owner. A proper condo policy is inexpensive and closes these gaps completely.
