Ontario specifics

What Ontario Drivers & Owners Should Know

Ontario trucking rates are driven by CVOR record, radius of operation, commodities hauled and driver experience — new authorities pay the most and need a broker who can actually place them. US exposure, reefer breakdown and cargo theft are the claims that sink unprepared operations. Sunny reviews renewals 60–90 days early, because trucking renewals left to the last minute almost always cost more.
