Ontario specifics

What Ontario Riders Should Know

Ontario motorcycle premiums are driven by bike class (sport bikes cost the most), engine size, your M-class experience, age, and riding history. New riders and sport-bike owners get the steepest quotes, but insurers disagree wildly on motorcycle pricing, so shopping 60+ markets finds outliers with far better rates. Completing an approved training course (like RTI or Humber) unlocks meaningful discounts with most insurers.
