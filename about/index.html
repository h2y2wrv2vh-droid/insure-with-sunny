Why RIBO Matters

Registered Insurance Brokers of Ontario licensees meet strict education, ethics and continuing-training requirements, and answer to a professional regulator. When you buy through a RIBO broker, you get accountability that direct sellers don't offer.

Licensed & Regulated

RIBO licensing means tested knowledge, ethical duties to clients, and ongoing professional education.
