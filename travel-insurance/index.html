Ontario specifics

What Ontario Drivers & Owners Should Know

OHIP pays only a small fraction of out-of-province emergency costs, the rest is on you without travel insurance. Super Visa applicants must show at least $100,000 in emergency medical coverage from a Canadian insurer. And if you have a pre-existing condition, the 'stability period' clause is the single most important thing to get right before you buy.
